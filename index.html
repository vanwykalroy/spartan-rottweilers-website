About Us

Two decades of careful, deliberate breeding

My journey began in 2003 when I received my very first Rottweiler, Nige, as a gift from my parents. I spent the next two years studying Rottweiler genetics, researching bloodlines, and learning from experienced breeders. In 2005 I purchased my first KUSA registered foundation female, Legion Chevy of Spartan — bred via artificial insemination from imported and South African champion bloodlines, with sound genetics, exceptional structure, and clear rich mahogany markings.

Chevy became the cornerstone of our breeding program. Through her we produced several unforgettable KUSA registered dogs that shaped the direction of the kennel — her influence can still be seen in what we breed today.

Our Philosophy

Our goal is to produce healthy, sound, and well-tempered Rottweilers that remain true to the breed standard while making exceptional family companions, working dogs, and show prospects. Every breeding is carefully planned — if we feel uncertain, we would rather skip and plan the next six months with certainty.

What We Focus On

Health, temperament, and structure — the qualities that make the Rottweiler such a remarkable breed, preserved and passed down through every generation we breed.

A Working Breed

The Rottweiler descends from Roman mastiff-type dogs that accompanied legions across the Alps, settling in Rottweil, Germany as the Metzgerhund — the "butcher's dog" — prized for herding, pulling carts, and guarding.
