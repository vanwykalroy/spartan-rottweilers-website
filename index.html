About Us

Spartan Rottweilers was founded from a lifelong passion for the Rottweiler breed and a commitment to preserving and improving its quality for future generations.

My journey began in 2003 when I received my very first Rottweiler, Nige as a gift from my parents. After acquiring Nige, I spent the next two years studding rottweiler genetics, researching bloodlines, getting to know and learning from experienced breeders, and developing my knowledge of the breed. In 2005 I purchased my first KUSA Registered foundation female rottweiler Legion Chevy of Spartan. Chevy was bred through artificial insemination out of Ch Legion Anomaly and Ch Benno vh Folconsnest (Belguim) via frozen Semen and possessed sound genetics, exceptional structure, movement, and breed type. Chevy became the cornerstone of our breeding program. Through her, we bred several unforgettable KUSA registered dogs that helped establish Spartan Rottweilers and shaped the direction of our kennel - her influence can still be seen in what we breed today.

Our Philosophy

At Spartan Rottweilers, we are committed to breeding healthy, well-tempered Rottweilers with strong head type, substantial bone, and sound structure. Every breeding is carefully planned to preserve the breed standard and produce loyal family companions, capable working dogs, and quality show prospects.

A Working Breed

The Rottweiler descends from Roman mastiff-type dogs that accompanied legions across the Alps, settling in Rottweil, Germany as the Metzgerhund — the "butcher's dog" — prized for herding, pulling carts, and guarding.
